OUR STORY

Built by Experience. Defined by Details.

Superior Home Solutions TX was founded by two craftsmen with more than a decade of custom closet experience each. We met while working in the industry, where we discovered a shared appreciation for careful installation, thoughtful design, and getting the details right.

Our experience also extends to kitchen installation and finish carpentry. That background taught us to look beyond the cabinetry itself—to notice how it fits against the walls, how the trim comes together, and how comfortably the space works for the person using it.

Over the years, we’ve designed and installed closets, worked through difficult layouts, and made the small adjustments that make a finished project feel complete. We know that quality shows in the alignment of the doors, the fit of the trim, and a layout that makes everyday belongings easier to reach.

We started Superior Home Solutions TX to put our experience into a company of our own, serving homeowners across Central Texas. Our name is new. The hands and experience behind the work are not.
